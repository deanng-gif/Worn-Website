What is Worn?

Worn lets you try on clothes before you buy them. Snap a photo of any piece you're eyeing, in a store or online, and Worn uses AI to show you what it would look like on your own body.

No. 02

How it Works:

  1. I. Add a few photos of yourself, plus your height and weight.
  2. II. Upload pictures of clothes you find and like.
  3. III. Worn creates an AI rendering showing how they'd look and fit on you.
